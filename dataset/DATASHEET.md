# Datasheet · Paired corpus for legal domain adaptation, v1.0

*[Versão em português: `DATASHEET.pt.md`](DATASHEET.pt.md)*

Written in the *datasheets for datasets* format (Gebru et al., 2021) on 13 September 2026,
describing the exact state that fed the 180 trained conditions of the ablation study.

> **Version notice.** This is v1.0, frozen for reproducibility, and **not** the version
> recommended for new work. It has known defects, measured and listed in section 6. Version
> 2.0, under construction, repairs the four truncated statutes, filters case generation
> through retrieval, and verifies case citations. Anyone wanting to train should wait for
> v2.0; anyone wanting to reproduce the study needs this one.

---

## 1 · Motivation

**Why the dataset was created.** To answer a question the domain-adaptation literature does
not separate: how much of the gain comes from the *content* of the corpus and how much from
the *form* in which it enters training. Answering it requires two corpora that differ only
in form, and they did not exist.

**Who created it.** Eduardo Pacheco, as part of a master's degree at ICMC-USP, supervised by
Cibele Maria Russo.

**Funding.** Project's own resources; training ran on AWS EC2 instances contracted for the
purpose.

---

## 2 · Composition

The dataset has two parts, serving different training stages.

### 2.1 · Continued pre-training: the pair

Two files, **paired document by document**. The same appellate decision that enters one
enters the other; what changes is the form.

| arm | documents | tokens (Tucano) | tokens (Qwen) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `bruto_completo.jsonl` (raw) | 39,856 | 50.58 M | 65.66 M |
| `expandido_completo.jsonl` (cards) | 41,900 | 59.43 M | 74.82 M |

In the **raw** arm the document enters as collected and is cut by length when it exceeds the
window. The cut is blind and falls wherever it falls, including mid-sentence.

In the **card** arm the same document becomes a *card*: the original text is preserved
intact in a field of its own and receives layers generated by a teacher model, namely a
summary, an explanation, the practical effect, related provisions, and question-answer
pairs. Each layer is a node with an identifier and an explicit parent relation, so the cut
respects node boundaries: each piece carries the identification header plus a coherent
subset of the layers.

Composition by category, as a percentage of tokens:

| arm | case decisions | statutes | consolidated case law | enacted opinions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| raw | 78.3% | 8.9% | 9.1% | 3.7% |
| cards | 35.5% | 36.3% | 19.9% | 8.3% |

**The difference in composition is a consequence of form, not a choice.** A statute has few
tokens in the original and many once expanded; an appellate decision is already long and
expansion adds proportionally less. This is a limitation of the pair and is declared as
such: part of the effect attributed to form may be category mixture.

The four `casado_*` files are the slices actually consumed by each model family, truncated
to match the token budget of the smaller arm under that family's tokeniser. The raw arm runs
exactly one epoch; the card arm is truncated to 0.851 epochs (Tucano) and 0.877 (Qwen).
**Neither arm repeats a document.**

### 2.2 · Fine-tuning: the six blocks

| block | examples | steps | composition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| I | 1,007 | 189 | real Bar Examination material, official rubric as the response |
| II | 1,182 | 222 | generated documents (648) and converted first-phase questions (534) |
| III | 1,681 | 316 | block II plus two-hop material (500) |
| IV | 2,687 | 504 | all Bar material, official and generated |
| V | 223 | 42 | other legal career examinations, no Bar material |
| VI | 2,909 | 546 | block IV plus block V |

Each block ships with a `valid.jsonl` of about 5%. Step counts are not fixed: they are
computed so that every block receives exactly three epochs, by `steps = ceil(3n / 16)`.

**The blocks are not nested by construction.** II and III do not contain I; IV contains I
and III; VI contains IV and V. Nesting was verified by comparing examples by content rather
than by label, because a generated example inherits the identifier of the original that
seeded it.

### 2.3 · What is **not** here

The evaluation set, that is the 105 Bar Examination questions, the 940 criteria and the gold
standard of three lawyers, is **not** part of this deposit. It comes from the Rabula
benchmark and lives in the `benchmark-juizes` deposit. The separation is deliberate: the
examinations those questions come from were **excluded from the entire training corpus**,
and keeping the two in distinct deposits makes that exclusion verifiable rather than merely
promised.

## 3 · Collection

**Sources.** Federal and São Paulo state statutes from official sites; appellate decisions
and summary opinions from the Supreme Federal Court, the Superior Court of Justice and the
São Paulo State Court of Appeals, through public interfaces and the national judiciary's
DataJud service; examinations and grading rubrics published by the examining boards.

**Collection period.** August and September 2026. Supreme Court decisions cover 2010 onward.

**Sampling.** This is not a probability sample of anything. It is what the public interfaces
returned for the requested slices, and the coverage biases of those interfaces are inherited
without correction. See section 6.

**Human labour.** None in this part. Human annotation exists only in the evaluation set,
which is in another deposit.

---

## 4 · Preprocessing

The raw arm receives minimal cleaning: whitespace normalisation, removal of repository
headers and footers, and cutting by context window.

The card arm is generated by a commercial teacher model from a fixed layer schema per
document type. The generation prompt and the schema are in the code deposit.

Both arms preserve the original text. In the card arm it sits in a field of its own and is
not rewritten; what is generated are the layers around it.

---

## 5 · Uses

**Intended use.** Continued pre-training and fine-tuning of language models for legal tasks
in Portuguese, and, the use for which the pair was built, studies that need to separate form
from content while holding the underlying collection constant.

**Discouraged uses.** This dataset is **not** a source of legal information. The generated
layers contain error, including fabricated case citations at a measured rate (section 6). A
system answering legal queries from this material, without verification against an
authoritative source, will cite cases that do not exist.

**Use in evaluation.** Do not evaluate models trained on this corpus using this corpus. And
do not evaluate on the Rabula benchmark any model trained with examinations 39, 40 and 41,
which is precisely why they were excluded here.

## 6 · Known defects, and what each one costs

This section exists because the defects were found by measuring, and because their
downstream cost was quantified. Publishing them is more useful than repairing them silently
in a version nobody can compare against this one.

**Four statutes entered truncated or did not enter.** The federal statute collector stopped
early on four files.

| statute | articles in corpus | articles in statute | coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Civil Code | 57 | 2,046 | 3% |
| Child and Adolescent Statute | 9 | 267 | 3% |
| Ordinary Criminal Code | 0 | 361 | absent |
| Commercial Code | 0 | 913 | absent |

The other fifteen statutes have coverage between 89% and 108%; values above 100% come from
articles with a suffix, such as 5-A, that the nominal count does not anticipate. **The
measured cost:** the analysis by area of law found no harm in the affected areas, and
Criminal Law has the largest gain of all areas on discursive questions. What holds is the
negative claim, not an assertion about the mechanism. The re-collection of these four is
done and enters v2.0.

**Fabricated case citations in the card arm.** The teacher model was not instructed to
verify the references it produced. There are **4,423 cards** citing a case number with a
plausible and non-existent format, half of all cards that cite case law. Of the 21,912 case
citations in the corpus, 7 resolve against a real database.

**The measured cost, and this is the one that matters:** the fabrication reaches the trained
model's output. The rate of responses citing a non-existent case is **0.512%** for
conditions trained on the card arm, against 0.091% for the raw arm and 0.072% for the
no-pre-training control, roughly seven times the control. In absolute terms it is low; in
direction it is systematic.

**Colliding identifiers.** In 42 cases two cards for *different* articles receive the same
identifier. The cause is twofold: articles above 999 lost the thousands separator, so
`CPC:ART:0001` gathered articles 1, 1,000, 1,001 and 1,002, and articles with a suffix lost
the suffix, so `CLT:ART:0075` gathered 75, 75-A and 75-B. That is 42 out of 12,781.

**Incomplete pairing.** The document-by-document pairing is not perfect in every subset:
three of them sit at 0.5%, 0% and 0.9% verifiable correspondence. This does not invalidate
the pair in aggregate, since it was built from the same source table, but it means the claim
"the same document in both arms" is verifiable only in part.

**Supreme Court summary opinions without a number.** 778 lines of the raw arm carry `None`
in place of the opinion number. In the card arm the number was preserved across all 1,714
opinion cards. This is one more asymmetry between the arms and is a candidate explanation
for part of the card arm's advantage.

**Split cards.** 1,613 cards in the statute corpus do not fit the window and are split
across more than one line, with **zero duplicates**: no line repeats the content of another.
This is designed behaviour, not a defect, and is recorded here to explain why there are more
lines than cards.

## 7 · Distribution and licence

**The compilation** is under CC BY 4.0.

**Source material.** Under Brazilian law, statutory text and judicial decisions are not
protected by copyright (Law 9,610/1998, article 8, items IV and V), and examinations and
rubrics are published by the examining boards. **The generated layers** were produced by a
commercial language model, and their use is subject to that service's terms at the time of
generation; anyone redistributing should verify them.

**Integrity check.** The manifest `manifestos/corpus-treino.json` carries the path, size,
line count and SHA-256 of every file. Verify with
`python dataset/congela.py corpus-treino --conferir`.
