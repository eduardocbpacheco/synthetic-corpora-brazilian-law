# Synthetic corpora in the adaptation of language models to Brazilian law

*[Versão em português: `LEIAME.md`](LEIAME.md)*

Code and reproduction material for the paper of the same name, which measures three
decisions that the standard domain-adaptation recipe treats as one: **which** text enters
continued pre-training, **in what form** it enters, and **how much** supervised data
follows.

The design is factorial. Two pre-training corpora paired document by document, one of raw
legal text and one in which the same material was restructured into cards with layers
generated by a teacher model, plus a no-pre-training control, crossed with six fine-tuning
blocks of distinct composition and four base models from 1.5 to 14 billion parameters. That
gives 180 trained conditions, each evaluated on 105 real Bar Examination questions against
940 criteria from the examining boards' official rubric, over ten independent runs.

**Eduardo Pacheco** · Institute of Mathematics and Computer Sciences, University of São Paulo.
Paper co-authored with Cibele Maria Russo.

## Start here

**[`REPRODUCING.md`](REPRODUCING.md)** walks the five stages and names the exact script for
each one. If you want to check the results rather than rebuild the experiment, stage 4 needs
no GPU and no API key: it recomputes every number in the paper from the deposited judgments.

## What is here

| directory | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `pipeline/` | corpus collection and assembly, benchmark construction, provenance diagnosis |
| `augmentation/` | generation of the structured cards and the source crawlers |
| `aws/` | the chains that ran the 180 conditions: pre-training, fine-tuning, generation |
| `avaliacao/` | judging, scoring, per-class agreement, paired contrasts, complementarity |
| `dataset/` | freezing, manifests with checksums, the datasheet, packaging |
| `publicacao/` | the paper sources, in Portuguese and English, and the IOS Press conversion |
| `explorador/` | a local application to inspect the judgments beside the generated answers |

This is a curated selection, not the working archive. Only the path that produced what is in
the paper is here; `REPRODUCING.md` explains what was left out and why.

## The data

The corpora and the judgments are **not** in this repository. They total about 3 GB and live
in separate deposits, one per licence and lifecycle, each with a persistent identifier.

| deposit | content | size |
|---|---|---:|
| `corpus-treino` | the raw/structured pair and the six fine-tuning blocks | 255 MB |
| `avaliacao-ablacao` | the judgments of all 180 conditions and the answers they generated | 119 MB |
| `benchmark-juizes` | the verdicts of 31 candidate judges and the human gold standard | 23 MB |

`dataset/manifestos/` carries, for every file in every deposit, its path, size, line count
and SHA-256. That is what turns "the data is available" into "these exact bytes produced
these results".

**[`dataset/DATASHEET.md`](dataset/DATASHEET.md)** describes the training corpus in datasheet
form. Read its section on known defects before training on it: this is the version that
reproduces the study, not the one recommended for new work.
